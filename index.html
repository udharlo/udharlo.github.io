FAQ

Khata book app: common questions

Is Udhar Lo a loan app?

No. Udhar Lo is a khata book for recording your own business transactions. Udhar here means the credit you give your customers. The app does not give loans, credit lines or any banking or financial services, and it does not move money between users.

Can I make GST bills in Udhar Lo?

Yes. You can create GST and non-GST bills as PDF and share them on WhatsApp, SMS or email, or print them.

Is Udhar Lo free?

Yes. Udhar Lo is free to download and use from Google Play. There are no ads.

What is a digital khata book?

A digital khata book is an app that replaces the paper udhar register. You record credit given to customers and payments received, and the app keeps every balance up to date for you.

Where is my khata data stored?

Your ledger is stored on your own phone. If you turn on backup, a copy is saved in your own Google Drive. We do not store your transactions on our servers.

Does Udhar Lo work without internet?

Yes. You can add customers and record entries offline. Internet is needed only for Google Drive backup and for sending reminders on WhatsApp.

Can I send payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. From any customer's ledger you can share their balance or a payment reminder on WhatsApp or SMS in one tap.

Is Udhar Lo available in Hindi?

Yes. The app works in Hindi and English.

Can I move my khata to a new phone?

Yes. Back up to your Google Drive, then sign in with the same Google account on the new phone and restore.
