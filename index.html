Udhar Lo

A simple digital khata (ledger) app for shopkeepers and small businesses.

Get it on Google Play

What Udhar Lo does

Udhar Lo replaces the paper khata book. It helps you track how much money customers owe you and how much you owe others, all from your phone.

  • Record credit given (Diya) and payments received (Liya) for each customer.
  • See the outstanding balance of every customer at a glance.
  • Send payment reminders to customers on WhatsApp and SMS.
  • Create and share PDF ledger statements, invoices and visiting cards.
  • Add entries by voice.
  • Lock the app with a PIN or fingerprint.

Your data stays with you

All ledger data is stored locally on your device. We do not run servers that store your transactions, and we do not sell or share your data.

How Udhar Lo uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for backup:

  • Email and basic profile: to show which Google account your backups are linked to, and to pre-fill your name.
  • Google Drive (only files created by this app): to save your backup file to your own Google Drive and restore it when you reinstall or change phones. Udhar Lo cannot see any other files in your Drive.
