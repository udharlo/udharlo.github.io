What Udhar Lo does

Built for shopkeepers, small business owners, freelancers and personal bookkeeping.

₹

Credit and payment entries

Record credit given (Diya) and payments received (Liya) for every customer and supplier.

⚖

Live balances

See each customer's outstanding balance and your total receivables at a glance.

✉

Payment reminders

Send polite payment reminders to customers on WhatsApp and SMS.

⎙

PDF statements and invoices

Create and share ledger statements, invoices and digital visiting cards.

🎙

Voice entry

Add transactions by speaking instead of typing.

🔒

PIN lock

Protect your khata with an app PIN.

Your data stays on your phone. Udhar Lo stores your ledger on your device. We do not run servers that store your transactions, and we never sell or share your data.

How Udhar Lo uses your Google account

Signing in with Google is used only to back up and restore your khata in your own Google Drive.

Email and basic profile

Shows which Google account your backup is linked to and pre-fills your name during setup.

Google Drive (app-created files only)

Saves your backup file to a "Udhar Lo Backups" folder in your own Drive and restores it on a new phone. Udhar Lo cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
