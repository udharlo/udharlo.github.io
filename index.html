Important Notice: Udhar Lo is NOT a loan app

Udhar Lo is purely a digital bookkeeping and ledger register app for small business owners. In our app, "Udhar" strictly refers to counter-credit given by shopkeepers to their retail customers (दुकान का माल उधार देना). Udhar Lo does not offer loans, borrow or lend money, partner with NBFCs, or broker credit lines. There are no ads and no financial selling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about Udhar Lo

Everything you need to know about pricing, security, data ownership, and features.

Is Udhar Lo a loan app?

No. Udhar Lo is strictly a digital bookkeeping and ledger app for shopkeepers to record customer store credit. It does not provide loans, credit lines, borrow/lend money, or broker any financial services.

Is Udhar Lo free? Are there any ads?

Yes. Udhar Lo is 100% free to download and use on Google Play. There are zero ads, no subscriptions, and no hidden charges.

Where is my shop's khata data stored?

Your ledger is stored locally on your own phone. If you enable backup, an encrypted copy is saved directly to your personal Google Drive account. We do not store your transactions on developer servers.

Does Udhar Lo work without internet?

Yes. Udhar Lo works 100% offline. You can add customers, record credit/payment entries, and manage your inventory without an active internet connection.

Can I create GST and non-GST PDF bills?

Yes. Udhar Lo includes an invoice maker that creates professional PDF bills with shop information and item details, ready to share on WhatsApp or print.

Can I send payment reminders on WhatsApp?

Yes. In one tap from any customer ledger screen, you can send a polite payment reminder on WhatsApp with an attached PDF statement and your shop's UPI QR code.

Does Udhar Lo support Hindi?

Yes. The entire app supports Hindi and English, including hands-free voice entry in both languages.

Can I restore my khata on a new phone?

Yes. Simply backup to your Google Drive, sign in with the same Google account on your new device, and your entire khata restores in seconds.
