Delete Account and Data

Udhar Lo, developed by Suresh Vaishnav.

We don't hold your data on our servers. Udhar Lo has no online account stored with us. Your data lives on your phone and, if you use backup, in your own Google Drive. You can delete both yourself using the steps below.

Option 1: Delete data from your phone

  1. Open your phone's Settings and go to Apps.
  2. Select Udhar Lo, then Storage.
  3. Tap Clear data. You can also simply uninstall the app.

This permanently deletes all transactions, customers, products and settings stored on the phone. It cannot be undone.

Option 2: Delete your Google Drive backup

  1. Open Google Drive with the Google account you used in Udhar Lo.
  2. Find the folder named Udhar Lo Backups.
  3. Delete the folder, then empty it from Trash to remove it permanently.

Option 3: Disconnect your Google account

  1. In Udhar Lo, open Settings and disconnect your Google account. Or:
  2. Go to your Google Account permissions page.
  3. Select Udhar Lo and remove its access.

Option 4: Ask us for help

If you need help deleting your data, email us from the Google account you used in the app:

What gets deleted

  • Deleted: all ledger entries, customers, suppliers, products, business profile and settings on your phone, and the backup file in your Google Drive.
  • Retained: nothing. We do not keep copies of your data on our side.
