1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Udhar Lo mobile application ("Udhar Lo", "we", "our" or "us") uses and protects information when you use the app. By installing or using Udhar Lo, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information the app uses

Udhar Lo stores the following on your phone so the app can work. Except where stated below (optional Google sign-in and Google Drive backup), this information is never transmitted to us:

  • Profile and business details you enter: Name, phone number, business or shop name, address, business category, UPI ID and, if you make GST bills, your GSTIN.
  • Khata, billing and stock records you enter: Customer and supplier names and phone numbers, transaction amounts, credit (Diya) and payment (Liya) entries, notes, due dates, bills and invoices (items, quantities, rates and tax amounts), and product stock details.
  • Contacts (optional): Only when you choose to add a customer from your phone's address book, the app reads the name and phone number of the specific contact you select. The app does not read, upload or store your full contact list.
  • Voice input (optional): When you use hands-free voice entry, the app uses your microphone and your phone's speech recognition service to convert spoken words into transaction text.
  • Google account information (optional): If you choose to sign in with Google for cloud backup, the app receives your email address, name and profile photo, along with access to create and manage the app's backup file in your Google Drive. See section 4.

3. How we use information

We use information solely to deliver the app's offline bookkeeping features:

  • Maintain customer and supplier credit (Diya) and payment (Liya) balances with instant totals.
  • Create and share PDF ledger statements, GST/non-GST bills, invoices and business cards.
  • Send payment reminders to customers through WhatsApp or SMS, strictly when you choose to trigger them.
  • Back up and restore your ledger data using your own Google Drive account.
  • Convert voice input into transaction entries at your counter.
  • Protect access to your shop's khata with a 4-digit PIN lock or biometric authentication.

Strict Anti-Lending & Anti-Advertising Commitment: We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use your customer records for lending, credit scoring, lead generation or debt collection.

4. Google user data

When you enable Google Drive backup, Udhar Lo requests access via Google OAuth 2.0:

Permission What it is used for
.../auth/drive.file Create, read and update only the backup file created by Udhar Lo in your Google Drive. The app cannot view or edit any other files in your Drive.
.../auth/userinfo.email Display your Google email in the app's backup screen so you know which account holds your backup.
.../auth/userinfo.profile Display your name and profile picture on the backup screen.

Google API Services User Data Policy: Udhar Lo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

  • On your device: Transactions, customer accounts, bills, inventory items and settings are stored locally in the app's private SQLite database on your phone.
  • Google Drive backup: Cloud backups run only when you tap the backup button. The file is saved directly into your personal Google Drive and is secured by your Google account credentials.
  • App lock & biometrics: You can set an app PIN to prevent unauthorized opening. Your PIN is stored as a secure one-way hash. If enabled, biometric fingerprint or face unlock is handled securely by Android's BiometricPrompt API.
  • Secure sign-in: Authentication is handled through standard Google OAuth 2.0. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Your phone security: We recommend using a phone lock screen (PIN, fingerprint or pattern) alongside the app's PIN lock.

6. App permissions

Udhar Lo requests only the permissions necessary to operate its features:

Permission Purpose & Usage
READ_CONTACTS Allows you to pick an existing customer from your contacts instead of manually typing their name and number. Optional.
RECORD_AUDIO
MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS		 Used exclusively for speech-to-text voice entry of transactions in Hindi and English. Optional.
USE_BIOMETRIC Allows unlocking the app using your device's fingerprint or biometric face sensor. Optional.
BLUETOOTH_CONNECT
BLUETOOTH		 Used to connect a Bluetooth headset microphone for voice entry or connect a Bluetooth thermal receipt printer. Optional.
INTERNET Required for Google Sign-In, Google Drive backup/restore, and launching WhatsApp or browser links.

You can deny or revoke optional permissions at any time in your phone's Android settings. The rest of the app will continue to function normally.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In & Google Drive API: Used for optional cloud backup and restore.
  • Firebase (Google): Used solely to initialize core Google authentication libraries. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, AdMob, or any third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Speech Recognition Service: Handled by Android's built-in speech engine (typically Google Speech Services) to transcribe voice input. Depending on your phone settings, audio processing may happen on-device or via Google's servers.
  • WhatsApp & SMS: Opened via Android Intents only when you explicitly choose to send a payment reminder, invoice, or statement.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you decide to delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app storage removes all local records. Backups saved in your Google Drive remain under your control until you remove them. Detailed step-by-step instructions are available on our Account and Data Deletion page.

9. Children's privacy

Udhar Lo is intended strictly for retail businesses, shopkeepers and personal ledger bookkeeping. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
