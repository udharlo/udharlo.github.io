1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the Udhar Lo mobile application ("Udhar Lo", "we", "our" or "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the app. By installing or using Udhar Lo, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

  • Profile and business details that you enter: name, phone number, business or shop name, address, business category and UPI ID.
  • Khata and transaction records that you enter: customer and supplier names, amounts, credit and debit entries, notes, due dates and product or stock items.
  • Contacts (optional). Only when you choose to add a customer from your phone's contacts, the app reads the selected contact's name and phone number to create the ledger entry.
  • Voice input (optional). When you use voice entry, the app uses your microphone to convert your speech to text so it can fill in the transaction.
  • Google account information (optional). If you sign in with Google for backup, we receive your email address, name and profile photo, and access to files the app creates in your Google Drive. See section 4.

3. How we use information

We use information only to provide the app's features:

  • Maintain credit (Diya) and payment (Liya) balances for your customers and suppliers.
  • Create and share PDF ledger statements, invoices and visiting cards.
  • Send payment reminders to your customers through WhatsApp or SMS, when you choose to.
  • Back up and restore your data using your own Google Drive, when you choose to.
  • Process voice input into transaction entries.
  • Protect access to the app with your PIN.

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. Udhar Lo requests only the following:

PermissionWhy we need it
Email address and basic profile (name, photo)To show which Google account your backup is linked to and to pre-fill your name during setup.
Google Drive: drive.file (files created by this app only)To create a "Udhar Lo Backups" folder in your Drive, save your backup file there, and restore it on a new or reset phone. The app cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your backup is sent directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. It is not sent to or stored on any server run by us.
  • We do not share Google user data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can disconnect your Google account inside the app at any time, and revoke access from your Google Account permissions page.

Udhar Lo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

  • On your device. All transactions, contacts you added, products and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Google Drive backup. Backups run only when you use the backup feature. The backup file is stored in your own Google Drive account and is protected by your Google account security.
  • App lock. You can set a PIN to stop others from opening the app on your phone. Your PIN is stored as a one-way hash, not as plain text.
  • Secure sign-in. Google sign-in uses Google's OAuth 2.0. We never see your Google password.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Sign-In and Google Drive API for optional backup and restore.
  • Firebase Core (Google) to initialize Google services in the app. We do not use Firebase Analytics or any advertising SDK.
  • Your phone's speech recognition service (on most Android phones this is provided by Google) to convert voice input to text.
  • WhatsApp and your SMS app, opened only when you choose to send a reminder.

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all local data. Backups in your Google Drive remain until you delete them. Step-by-step instructions are on our Account and Data Deletion page.

9. Children's privacy

Udhar Lo is meant for business and personal bookkeeping and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
