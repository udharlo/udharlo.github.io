2. The service

Udhar Lo is a digital ledger (khata), billing and stock management app designed to help shopkeepers, small business owners, traders, freelancers and individuals record and track customer credit, payments, bills, invoices, stock inventory and counter transactions.

3. Udhar Lo is not a lender

Udhar Lo is strictly a record-keeping tool. "Udhar" in the app refers exclusively to customer counter credit extended by you to your own buyers and recorded in your shop's khata.

  • Udhar Lo does not provide personal loans, business loans, credit lines, money lending, peer-to-peer lending, or any banking or financial services.
  • Udhar Lo does not move money between users.
  • UPI QR codes displayed in the app simply display your own UPI details so your customers can scan and pay you. Actual money transfers happen inside your customer's third-party UPI app (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm), not within Udhar Lo.
